ACADEMIC FOUNDATION

The Rooms Division Discipline

The Rooms Division is the financial engine and heartbeat of any luxury hotel property, encompassing Front Desk leadership, Concierge, Guest Relations, and Executive Housekeeping.

Front Office Excellence

From check-in orchestration to high-stakes guest recovery, training students to personify flawless poise, cultural empathy, and proactive guest delight.

  • VIP Protocol & High-Profile Arrival Staging
  • Property Management Systems (Opera PMS / Fidelio)
  • Dynamic Room Upselling & Revenue Optimization

Accommodation Operations

Instilling the rigorous international standards of luxury suites, hygiene microbiology, linen science, and sustainable hotel practices.

  • Presidential Suite Inspection & Turn-Down Protocols
  • Eco-certified Cleaning & Fabric Care Science
  • Room Inventory Audits & Preventive Maintenance

Revenue & Yield Dynamics

Bridging operational charm with analytical decision-making to maximize RevPAR (Revenue Per Available Room) and Average Daily Rate (ADR).

  • RevPAR, TrevPAR & GOPPAR Analytics
  • Demand Forecasting & Overbooking Risk Balancing
  • Online Travel Agency (OTA) Channel Management
